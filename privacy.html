Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Data accessed

The app accesses files in the user's Google Drive only to synchronize the user's Obsidian vault between the user's devices and the user's own Google Drive.

Use and storage

Data is transferred only between the user's devices and Google Drive. The app does not operate any server, and no data is collected, stored, or processed by the developer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Please open an issue at this repository.

本アプリは、ユーザーの Obsidian Vault をユーザー自身の端末と Google Drive の間で同期する目的でのみ Google Drive のファイルにアクセスします。開発者はサーバーを運用せず、データを収集・保存・第三者提供しません。アクセス権は Google アカウントの権限設定からいつでも取り消せます。
